📖 Grammar Hints for Flyers

  • Past Continuous: Actions happening in the past (was reading, were playing).
  • Present Perfect: Experiences up to now (has just finished, have you ever been).
  • Future Forms: Predictions and plans (will stay, won't come).
  • Relative Pronouns: Linking ideas (The boy who, The book which).
  • Modals of Advice: You should / You ought to study hard.

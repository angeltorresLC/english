🧩 Grammar Syntax

Show how good you are at building sentences! We will practice the Past Continuous, Comparatives, and Future tenses.

Important Flyers Rules

  • Comparatives: Use -er or more (e.g., bigger, more beautiful) to compare two things.
  • Past Continuous: Use was/were + verb(ing) for long actions in the past (e.g., I was watching TV).
  • Future Plans: Use going to for plans you have already made.

✍️ Use of English & Punctuation

Punctuation and word order are super important! Make sure you know when to use capital letters, full stops (.), and question marks (?).

Punctuation Rules

  • Always use a Capital Letter for the start of a sentence, names of people, and days of the week.
  • Put a Question Mark (?) at the end if you are asking something.
  • Make sure adjectives come before the noun (e.g., a red apple).

My Passion for Underwater Photography

By Lucas Wright


I’ve always loved swimming, but two years ago, my uncle gave me an underwater camera for my birthday. That gift changed everything. At first, taking good pictures was surprisingly difficult. Water distorts light, and fish move fast! My initial attempts resulted in blurry images of fish tails.

I decided to take a diving course to improve my technique. Learning to stay perfectly still underwater made a huge difference. Now, I spend hours exploring marine reserves near my home, capturing images of colorful coral and marine life. My main advice to beginners is to be patient—you can't rush nature.

What I enjoy most is the silence down there. In our busy world, it's rare to find a place where you only hear your own breathing. Some people find the ocean scary, but to me, it feels like a second home. I hope my photos encourage more people to support ocean conservation efforts because these ecosystems are fragile.

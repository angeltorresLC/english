Part 3: Multiple Choice (Longer Text)

My First Marathon, by Sarah (16)
"I decided to run a marathon because my older brother did one last year. I thought it looked easy when he crossed the finish line. However, the training was incredibly difficult. Waking up at 5 AM in the freezing cold was the worst part. My parents were worried I was pushing myself too hard, but my coach told me I was doing great. During the actual race, I wanted to stop at mile 15, but seeing the crowd cheering kept me going. I didn't win, but finishing was the best feeling ever."

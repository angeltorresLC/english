🎯 Desempeños (Performances)

🧠 3.1 Grammar & Vocabulary Talks about the sequence of events, actions done by others, shifts focus away from a subject, and defines specific people, places, and concepts using past perfect tenses, causative structures, the passive voice, relative clauses, and articles. Focuses on vocabulary related to school, travel, social media, and the natural/artificial worlds.
📖 3.2 Reading Comprehension Demonstrates reading comprehension by recognizing the purpose and meaning of short texts, identifying main ideas, key details, and implied information in longer passages. Analyzes characters, events, and themes making logical connections supported by textual evidence.
🎧 3.3 Listening Comprehension Comprehends longer spoken texts by identifying key points, detailed information, the speaker's purpose, and implied meaning. Improves skills in following sequences of events, recognizing transitions, and interpreting opinions or attitudes.
✍️ 3.4 Writing Skills Writes effective stories by adjusting language to purpose, sequencing ideas logically, and using appropriate grammar and vocabulary. Develops skills in expressing personal experiences, describing events, and using narrative features.
🗣️ 3.5 Speaking Skills Talks about trips or memorable events using organized ideas, time markers, and descriptive expressions. Strengthens skills in discussing ideas with a partner, suggesting alternatives, and reaching simple agreements.
🤝 3.6 Responsibility (Classwork) Develops proposed activities in a responsible and organized manner, showing interest and compliance in delivering them within the established time.

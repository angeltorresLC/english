Useful Signposting Words

Words and phrases that guide your audience through a text or speecH - Road signs - These linguistic tools connect ideas, signal transitions, and help the listener or reader anticipate what comes next.

Why Signposting Matters
Without clear signposts, writing or presentations can feel disjointed and confusing.
Proper signposting ensures clarity, improves logical flow, and makes information much easier to follow.
Introductions: Tell the audience exactly what to expect and outline your main points.
Transitions: Bridge the gap between paragraphs or speaking points so the argument moves smoothly.
Sequencing: Show order or chronology using markers.
Summarizing: Wrap up arguments and recap key takeaways before a conclusion.

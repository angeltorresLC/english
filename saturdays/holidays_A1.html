🏖️ Holiday Expedition Briefing

🎯 Mission Objective

Welcome, traveler! Your objective is to navigate the holiday vocabulary landscape. Along the way, you will acquire, decrypt, and demonstrate mastery over A1 beginner vocabulary, essential travel words, and reading comprehension required for confident English conversations about holidays.

🧠 Skills Involved

  • Contextual Interpretation: Aligning basic travel phrases accurately with contextual cues.
  • Synonym Mapping: Synthesizing simple alternative words to basic holiday terms.
  • Syntactic Precision: Mastering beginner grammar anchors and syntax placements under structural rules.
  • Reading Comprehension: Sifting through short travel logs to process basic textual statements.

📊 Scoring & Vital Systems

  • XP Points (Score): Awarded generously upon successfully validating linguistic questions.
  • O2 / HP Levels: Your life support system starts at 100. Any structural mismatch or contextual error triggers an environmental drop (-10 to -15 O2/HP) and docks -10 XP.
  • System Failure: Letting O2 hit 0% results in missing your flight. Retrying a stage costs 500 XP.

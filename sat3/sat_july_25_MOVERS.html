🧩 Great Grammar

Show how amazing you are at making sentences! We will look at things that happened yesterday and compare big and small things.

Important Movers Rules

  • Comparatives: Add -er to short words to compare two things (e.g., A mouse is smaller than an elephant).
  • Past Simple: Use was/were or words with -ed for things that happened yesterday!
  • Have got / Has got: We use this to talk about things we own. (e.g., She has got a new bike!)

✍️ Super Sentences

Using capital letters and question marks is super important! Let's fix these mixed-up sentences.

Sentence Rules

  • Start every sentence with a BIG Capital Letter!
  • Put a Question Mark (?) if you are asking something!
  • Put a Full Stop (.) when you finish telling something!
